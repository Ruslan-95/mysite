About me

I'm an engineering leader with 10+ years in software. I head Software Engineering at Bir ecosystem — Kapital Bank's digital business in Baku — leading a 100+ person organisation of developers, QA engineers and analysts across three product tribes. My scope is hiring, capability development, and engineering practices and standards. Delivery is owned by the tribes; I own the organisation that makes it possible.

I was promoted from Director of QA CoE after building the bank's quality organisation from scratch: company-wide Quality Gates and Zero Bug Policy that cut critical production incidents by 50%+, test automation scaled from 0% to 40% of the core regression suite, and a capability programme that trained and certified 40+ engineers. Before that I built and scaled engineering quality at Sber, Yandex, VK and international scale-ups.
